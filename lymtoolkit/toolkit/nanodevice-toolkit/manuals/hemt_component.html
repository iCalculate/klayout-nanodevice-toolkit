NANODEVICE FUNCTION MANUAL

HEMT Device

HEMT 器件：支持 S-G-D 与 S-G-D-G-S 核心、Mesa、介质、粗细 EBL 分层扇出及对准标记。Creates S-G-D or S-G-D-G-S HEMTs with mesa, dielectric, split fine/coarse EBL fanout, and alignment marks.
